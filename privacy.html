Privacy policy

Last update: 23 November 2026

Saber Wallet ("Saber") is a self-custody wallet for the Solana blockchain. Self-custody means that only you hold the keys. This policy tells which data Saber uses and where the data goes.

Summary

  • Saber keeps your recovery phrase and private keys on your device, encrypted. Saber does not send them anywhere.
  • The Saber project has no server. Saber does not collect analytics and does not track you.
  • To show balances, Saber sends your public wallet address to the blockchain services in the list below.

Data that stays on your device

  • Recovery phrase and private keys. Saber encrypts them with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key comes from your password. Saber keeps the encrypted data in the storage of the extension in your browser.
  • Unlocked keys. When you unlock the wallet, Saber keeps the keys in the session memory of the browser. Auto-lock and Lock clear them. Chrome also clears them when you close the browser.
  • Settings and cached data. Account names, settings, cached balances, and your transaction history stay in the storage of the extension.

Data that Saber sends

A public wallet address is visible to all people on the Solana blockchain. To show balances and to send transactions, Saber sends your public wallet address to these services:

  • The Solana RPC services on your list. The default is PublicNode. You can add your own, for example Helius.
  • Jupiter: token balances, prices, and swaps.
  • Raydium and DexScreener: prices, and swaps when Jupiter is busy.
  • Phoenix: perps markets and your perps account.
  • Kamino, Meteora, Orca, and Raydium: DeFi positions.
  • Magic Eden: NFTs.
  • Stakewiz: the list of validators.

These services can also see your IP address. Each service has its own privacy policy. When you approve a transaction, Saber sends the signed transaction to the Solana network through your RPC or through the swap service.

Data that Saber does not collect

Saber does not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage data. Saber does not show advertisements. Saber does not sell, rent, or share data with any person.

Permissions

Saber asks Chrome for these permissions:

  • storage and unlimitedStorage: to keep the encrypted wallet, the settings, and the transaction history on your device.
  • sidePanel: to show the wallet in the Chrome side panel.
  • alarms: to lock the wallet after the auto-lock time, and to load the transaction history in the background.
  • Access to the services in the list above: to read balances and to send transactions that you approve. Saber asks for access to other addresses only when you add your own RPC.

Remove your data

Select Settings → Remove wallet from this browser, or remove the extension. Chrome then deletes all data of the extension. Keep your recovery phrase offline, because Saber cannot restore your wallet without it.

Children

Saber is not for people younger than 18 years.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version shows on this page with a new date.
