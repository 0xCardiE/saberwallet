01 · Swap

Swap any Solana token

Jupiter finds the route across Solana exchanges. If Jupiter is busy, Saber gets a quote from Raydium.

  • See the rate, the minimum amount, and the price impact before you swap.
  • Saber simulates the transaction first, and blocks it if it takes more than the swap amount.
02 · Stocks

Hold real stocks on Solana

xStocks are tokens that follow the price of one real share. Buy them with USDC at any time of day.

  • NVIDIA, Tesla, Apple, the S&P 500, the Nasdaq, gold, and more.
  • Your stocks show next to your tokens, with the 24-hour change.
03 · Perps

Trade perps on Phoenix

Phoenix runs on the Solana blockchain, so your collateral stays on Solana.

  • Crypto, stocks, and commodities, with up to 100× leverage.
  • Each position shows its PnL, margin, and liquidation price. Close it with one transaction.
04 · Staking

Stake SOL your way

Earn staking rewards in two ways.

  • Liquid staking: get JupSOL, JitoSOL, mSOL, or INF, and swap back at any time.
  • Native staking: choose a validator by commission and APY. Unstake and withdraw from the same screen.
Security and privacy

Your keys stay with you

Saber is a self-custody wallet. Only you hold the keys, and the Saber project has no server.

Encrypted on your deviceAES-256-GCM, with a key from your password. Saber never sends your recovery phrase or keys.
Transaction guardBefore you sign a swap or a perps order, Saber simulates it. If it takes more than you approved, Saber blocks it.
Auto-lockSaber clears the keys from memory after a time without activity. You choose the time.
No analyticsNo analytics, no advertisements, no account. Read the privacy policy.
FAQ

Questions

Is Saber free?

Yes. Saber does not charge a fee. You pay only the Solana network fees, and the fees of the exchange that you use, for example Jupiter or Phoenix.

Can I import my Phantom or Solflare wallet?

Yes. Import your recovery phrase to see the same accounts. You can also import one private key from Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack.

Where are my keys?

On your computer only, encrypted with your password. If you lose the password and the recovery phrase, nobody can restore the wallet. Keep the recovery phrase offline.

Do I need my own RPC?

No. Saber works with free public services. If you add a free key from a provider such as Helius, Saber loads data faster and shows more NFTs.

Can I use perps and stocks in my country?

Phoenix perps are not available in the United States or in sanctioned regions. xStocks are not available to US persons. Check the rules in your country before you trade.

Is Saber safe for large amounts?

Saber is new software. Use it for amounts that you can afford to lose, and keep large amounts in a hardware wallet.
